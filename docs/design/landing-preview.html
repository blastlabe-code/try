How it works

Three steps from the dock to your wallet.

Step 1

Buy a rod

Pay in $GAME, the game token launched on pons.family. Your rod is an NFT with a durability counter, so you can keep it, sell it, or repair it later.

Step 2

Cast

Each cast uses one point of durability and starts a cooldown on that rod. Chainlink VRF supplies the randomness, so nobody can pick the catch, including us.

Step 3

Reel in and claim

Fish pay $GAME and stock fish pay stock tokens straight into your rewards. Claim whenever you like. Claims keep working even if the game is paused.

Why the pond never runs dry

No prize is a fixed amount. Each one is a small slice of what the pool holds at that moment, so the game can never owe more than it has.

prize = pool × share × rod multiplier
// capped at 5% of the pool per catch

When many people play, pools fill faster than they pay out and prizes grow. When play slows, pools shrink slowly and never hit zero. Stock splits and price moves don't matter either, because prizes are counted in tokens.

What comes back to players

Over time the pools pay out what flows in. That makes the long-run return easy to state: the share of spending routed to prize pools.

    Fair by design

    Rules you can read on-chain.

    Verifiable randomness

    Every catch comes from a Chainlink VRF proof. Robinhood Chain has no safe built-in randomness, so the game never uses block data.

    Your winnings are yours

    Prizes are credited to you inside the prize vault. The admin has no function that can move credited funds.

    Seven-day warning

    Pool funds that are not yet won can only leave after a public seven-day delay, so players always see it coming.

    No spam casting

    Every rod has a cooldown and a fixed number of casts. Cooldowns stay with the rod when it is traded.

    Price change limits

    Rod prices can move at most 25% per day, and every purchase has a maximum price, so a price change can't catch you mid-purchase.

    Open source

    Contracts, tests and the economy simulator are public. Run the simulator yourself to check the numbers on this page.

    FAQ

    Before you cast.

    Where do I get $GAME?

    $GAME launches on pons.family, the token launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Buy it there or on Uniswap once it trades, then connect the same wallet here.

    Which wallet and network do I need?

    Any EVM wallet that can add a custom network. Robinhood Chain uses chain ID 4663, and gas is paid in ETH. The app adds the network for you.

    Are stock tokens the same as owning shares?

    No. Robinhood Stock Tokens are tokenized debt instruments that track a stock's value. They give economic exposure but no shareholder rights, and they are not offered to US persons or in several other jurisdictions.

    What happens when my rod breaks?

    At zero durability the rod stops casting. You can repair it twice. Each repair costs 75% of the rod's price per missing cast and lowers its maximum durability by 20%. Or keep it as a trophy.

    Can I lose money?

    Yes. This is a game of chance, and the average return is below what you spend. Only play with money you are comfortable losing, and check that games like this are legal where you live.

    Who can change the rules?

    Settings sit behind a multisig with a timelock, and hard limits are built into the contracts. Changes emit public events, and the app shows the current values on the Rules page.
